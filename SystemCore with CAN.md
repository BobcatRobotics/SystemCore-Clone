## Getting Started

1. Download the systemcore-can-nofd.img.zip
2. Unzip the image
3. Using raspberry pi imager or balena etcher to burn the image to an SD card
4. Put SD Card into the pi and boot
5. Boot the raspberry pi
6. Connect to the SYSTEMCORE wifi that is generated by the PI
7. open an browser and navigate to either 172.30.0.1 or robot.local
7. YOUR IN!
